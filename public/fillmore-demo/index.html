Fillmore demo forms

  1. Install Fillmore from the Chrome Web Store, or load fillmore/extension/dist unpacked.
  2. Click the Fillmore icon in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+F) to open the side panel.
  3. On the Documents tab, press Load sample documents (Asha Rao's passport, payslip PDF and electricity bill, all fictional).
  4. Open a form below and click the Fillmore icon on it. Answer the questions in the panel, then press Fill. Submitting goes nowhere.
  5. For the penguin, allow Fillmore on this site once (Form tab → Always allow); it then appears on these pages by itself.
